Our Story

💛 How It All Began

This company was born from a simple moment with my own children.

I went out looking for colouring books for my kids — they love creating, and I wanted something beautiful for them to enjoy. But everywhere I looked, it was the same: mythical creatures, cartoon characters from TV shows, nothing fresh, nothing meaningful.

And then it came to me: Wouldn't it be wonderful to have something that sparks their creativity AND points them to God?

Why should choosing between fun and faith be necessary? Why can't they colour, draw, and create — and in the same moment, learn about the One who made them?

That is the heart of Grace Line Arts: creativity that matters, learning that lasts, and every page a reminder of God's love.

Based in Birmingham, United Kingdom — reaching families everywhere with faith-filled creative resources.

Our name says everything: Grace — rooted in God's free gift of love; Line — every stroke, puzzle piece, and activity draws young hearts closer to Him.
